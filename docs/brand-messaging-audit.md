# PUNTR brand messaging and copy alignment audit

Audit baseline: 22 November 2026  
Scope: public marketing pages, navigation, referral messaging, commercial disclosures and upcoming product pages in `jobbyist/punthubv2`.

## Canonical positioning

**PUNTR is South Africa's sports prediction, insights and community platform.**

The product helps sports fans publish timestamped predictions before kick-off, receive transparent grading and build a public performance record. The core proposition is accountability: picks are locked, results are graded consistently, and the record includes wins and losses.

PUNTR is not a bookmaker. It does not accept bets, set bookmaker odds, hold betting funds or facilitate wagering on the platform. Third-party bookmakers remain separate businesses.

## Message hierarchy

1. **Core promise:** Predict smarter. Prove it.
2. **Proof:** Timestamped picks, locked at kick-off, transparent grading and a public record.
3. **Community:** Learn, compete and share sports knowledge with other fans.
4. **Free entry:** Joining and core community participation are free. Paid products must be described separately and only as available when enabled.
5. **Commercial value:** Puntr Pro is a paid subscription product; the current planned price is R99/month. Do not imply checkout is available while payments are disabled.
6. **Referral value:** Approved referrers can earn 10–30% recurring commission on eligible Puntr Pro subscription revenue from successful paid referrals. Free registration alone does not earn a Pro commission. Rates and payment terms must be confirmed in the approved programme agreement.
7. **PuntPoints:** Non-cash loyalty points. Never describe them as cash, betting credit or withdrawable funds.
8. **Responsible play:** 18+ only. Puntr promotes safer gambling and links to independent support resources. Do not suggest Puntr itself provides counselling or bookmaker self-exclusion.

## Approved copy principles

- Lead with sports knowledge, transparent records and community; do not lead with the prospect of betting profits.
- Use “prediction”, “pick”, “record”, “performance” and “community” for Puntr's own product.
- Use “bet”, “wager” and “bookmaker” only when clearly describing third-party activity.
- Do not promise prediction accuracy, guaranteed outcomes, income, winnings or a financial edge.
- Distinguish live features from roadmap items. Use “in development”, “planned” or “coming soon” until a feature is available and verified.
- Clearly label affiliate links, paid placements, sponsored rewards and ambassador content.
- Commercial relationships must never be presented as influencing predictions, grading, rankings or editorial decisions.
- Keep 18+ and responsible-play notices visible wherever bookmaker links, betting promotions or gambling-related partner campaigns appear.

## Commercial programme rules to communicate

### Referrals
- Commission range: 10–30% recurring.
- Commission basis: eligible Puntr Pro subscription revenue generated by a successfully referred member who becomes a paying Pro subscriber.
- No Pro subscription commission is earned for a free registration alone.
- The individual rate, attribution rules, qualifying events, reversals, reporting, payment cadence, tax treatment and termination conditions must be defined in the final programme terms or partner agreement. Do not invent these details in public copy.
- Referral commissions relate to Puntr subscription revenue, not bookmaker deposits, stakes, losses, winnings or betting activity.

### Brand ambassadors
- Open to eligible 18+ creators, influencers, tipsters and community representatives.
- Selection includes a 30–45 day assessment period.
- Selected ambassadors may receive a fixed monthly retainer, performance bonuses/commissions, vouchers, merchandise and partner-sponsored giveaways.
- A retainer or reward is not guaranteed by applying or entering the assessment period. Scope, amount, deliverables and payment terms are confirmed in writing.
- Ambassadors must follow responsible-play, advertising-disclosure and brand-safety requirements.

### Fantasy Arena
- Fantasy Arena v3.0 is a planned dedicated product, not the same thing as the current Practice Mode.
- Describe fantasy-style leagues, head-to-head challenges and seasonal leaderboards as planned capabilities until released.
- Current Practice Mode remains a fictional simulation with demo points and no cash value.

## Bookmaker directory provenance

The 43 displayed names and licence references are a curated snapshot from the [National Gambling Board Verified Operators register](https://www.ngb.org.za/verified-operators/), which states that its data comes from Provincial Licensing Authorities and was last updated 16 November 2026. Some records are brand names; others are licence-holder names. A listing is not proof that the brand's website is approved for online betting in every province. The page directs users back to the NGB register, contains no direct betting links and warns users to verify current status. This follows the NGB disclaimer that the register is not intended to advertise gambling. This directory must be refreshed against the regulator source before any operator is promoted or monetised.

**Known reconciliation item:** the NGB snapshot lists BET.co.za under licence reference 10145732-017. Do not use that reference in paid promotional material until it has been reconciled with the current WCGRB record and the operator's published licence documents.
